## Description (1382 / 4000)
Without the chat line: on iOS the chat is hidden in PRO until report and block exist (D-13).
```
Squadfy runs your amateur football group's weekly match. No spreadsheets, no lists in the group chat, no "who's missing?".

AUTOMATIC SIGN-UPS
• Pick your match day and time: it repeats every week.
• Sign-ups open on their own the day after the match and close before the next one.
• Join in one tap. When it's full you go on the waiting list and move up automatically when someone drops out.
• Holiday or pitch unavailable? Cancel or move that week with a reason, and the whole club sees it right away.

FAIR TEAMS, NO ARGUMENTS
• Automatic draw by each player's level and position.
• See both teams on the pitch and how even they are.

THE MATCH REPORT
• Log goals, assists, cards and minutes played.
• Every player's rating adjusts match by match.

STANDINGS BY SEASON
• Each season runs from September to June; your club picks the exact day.
• Rating, top scorers, assists, matches and wins tables.
• Closed seasons are kept for good.

ALWAYS UP TO DATE
• Notifications when sign-ups open, teams are published or you get a spot.
• Mute a club whenever you like.

YOUR CLUB, IN A MINUTE
• Create the club and invite your people with a code.
• Captain and admin roles to share the work.
• Sign in with email, Google or Apple.

Your data is yours: no ads, and crash reports and usage statistics are only on if you turn them on. You can delete your account from the app at any time.
```
